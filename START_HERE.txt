generated training raises mean full-event recovery from 30.2 to 62.5 per 128-root
block, with new damage falling from 2.08 to 0.00. Recovery improves in all eight

benefit. At 256 bundles, augmentation has worse mean F1, recovery and damage in
both sources at both tested targets.

exposes residual harm: none of four source/training combinations establishes
beneficial merging without added damage. True identity recovery and history F1
can improve at a cost. Never merge can still introduce wrong participation links.
