This study freezes 36 previously fitted models, their real-data budgets and
calibration thresholds. It adds no generated examples or fitting labels.
The protocol was written before selecting or scoring the new histories.

Freshness: the source adapter reconstructs all previous raw root selections.
It excludes original feasibility prefixes and original confirmation histories,
then extends the revision ranking AFTER development512, annotation1024,
confirmation1024 and the expanded native2048 blocks. All four previous raw root
selection hashes must reproduce. The next1024 roots are divided into eight
128-root blocks by a fixed outcome-independent hash. Target roots and children
are disjoint across prior/new partitions and blocks. Context resources/vendors
can overlap. This is new-history confirmation in previously studied source
processes, not independent institutions, natural errors, or new source schemas.

Public reference histories are treated as clean for controlled fault injection.
The verified real fitting/calibration budget remains16/64/256 per model, split
75%/25%, with identical real training arrays in both arms. The 36 saved models
were trained before this protocol. All three seeds and both precision targets
are retained. The primary grid comprises576 conditions. The safety comparison
adds192 conditions (unguarded and never_merge at budget64,target.99), for768.
No-edit predictions are also scored for all48 block/fault configurations.

Never-merge uses the exact guarded decoder with an infinite merge threshold.
It retains addition tie rejection and atomic root replacement, so only merge
eligibility changes. It still repairs participation and can introduce wrong
links. Its zero false-merge count does not imply zero event damage.

Hypotheses and interval rules are frozen in protocol.json. Paired comparisons
average three fixed fitted-model/fault runs per block, then resample the eight
blocks10000 times. These conditional percentile intervals summarize sampled
history variation within the same source process. They do not measure uncertainty
over training populations or independent institutions, and are not adjusted for
all secondary comparisons. All results, including failures, must be reported.

Only one worker per domain. Workers may execute concurrently, with four CPU
threads each. Completed results check input/model/artifact hashes before reuse.
Existing frozen implementations and earlier experimental results stay unchanged.
The analyzer replays every edit, independently recomputes history F1, full-event
recovery and damage including false-merge contamination, and verifies exact undo
and immutable event content. The final review requires a current PDF visual audit.

Artifacts:
