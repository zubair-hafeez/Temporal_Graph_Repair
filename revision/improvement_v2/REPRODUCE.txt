Question
Does generated supervision improve repair beyond eight distinct corruptions of
exactly the same verified real histories? Budgets 16/64/256 include calibration.
The two arms use byte-identical real fitting and calibration arrays. History
verification is simulated access to public reference representations; it is not
investigator annotation. Roles are root/child-disjoint and nested by budget.

Scope
This is an exploratory follow-up on 128 hash-selected roots from each previously
used confirmation cohort. Three seeds vary allocation and faults, not institutions
or source processes. A new frozen protocol records choices before new model
outcomes, but does not turn reused source cohorts into independent confirmation.
One simultaneous edit pass with atomic root substitutions differs from the old
four-pass study. Do not directly compare its scores to the old 1024-root results.
Only selected verified histories enter real fitting/calibration graph context.
Both arms have identical real examples and differ by added generated examples;
total training rows and compute are intentionally measured rather than equalized.

Generated execution
32 executable worlds, 16 roots each, with root/item/resource structure, varying
arrival overlap, resource queues, lognormal durations, cancellations, retries,
coarse timestamp recording and varying noisy fields. Eight independently seeded
recording conditions cover mixed, independent, mild, severe, missing-dominant,
wrong-dominant, fragmentation-dominant and pipeline faults. Two additional empty
history graphs represent unresolved ambiguity, with no uniquely justified edit.
They have multiple compatible latent truths, not an arbitrarily chosen positive.
The ambiguity stress test is narrow: indistinguishable empty catalog histories.
No claim of natural incident rates, calibrated digital twins, or comprehensive
ambiguity detection is made. Individual generator mechanisms are not ablated.

Information ablations
At budget64, retrain generated-plus-real using no timestamps, no context objects,
no descriptive fields, or no history edges. Projection precedes candidate
retrieval, index construction and fitting. No-context retains the root/child
incidence needed to represent target histories; no-history removes all incidence
from the evidence index, retaining the current association only to define an edit
and its current cardinality. This is an information study with boosted scorers,
not evidence that a graph neural architecture is necessary or superior.

Safety
Fixed calibration precision targets .95/.99, minimum five correct actions per
operation. Insufficient support means abstention. Safe decoding rejects tied
additions, co-occurring alias merges, merge scores below .99, and merges that are
not mutual best with a .05 margin. The exactly-one-root contract is supplied by
the source schema. It cannot certify the identity of the selected replacement.
No-threshold tuning uses test labels. Recovery/damage operating points are
descriptive; a harm frontier is not a deployment guarantee or a new held-out
threshold-selection set. Original-event content is immutable. Saved edit programs
and prior-event/cluster dictionaries permit exact replay and undo.
