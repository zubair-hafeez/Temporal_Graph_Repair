AUTHOR REVIEW — COMPLETED REVISION

Read revision/paper/main.pdf: 13 total pages including references.
Title: Restoring Operational Histories with Generated Temporal Graph Supervision.

What the paper does
The system learns from generated workflow examples and a limited number of verified
public business histories to restore missing/wrong connections and fragmented IDs.
It preserves the recorded events and provides an audit trail of its changes.

Strongest result
For purchasing histories, generated-plus-real training with 64 complete verified
bundles achieves mean history F1 .8561, versus .8453 for real-only training with
256 bundles. It wins all three paired trials. Budgets include fitting and
calibration. This is one quarter as many verified bundles in this experiment;
it is not a measured fourfold reduction in annotation time.

Honest limits that remain in the paper
The mixed model beats generated-only purchasing repair in only one of three trials.
Loan gains depend on the mixture and threshold, and some settings lose to real-only.
Low-budget alias recovery is weak. Clean inputs can be damaged. Generated-only
purchasing results are poor in the separate missing-ID task. Main evaluation uses
controlled injected errors in public records, without independently verified
natural business-error truth. Existing classifiers are used; no new architecture
or universal state-of-the-art claim is made. These are material reviewer concerns.

What is complete
All 156 frozen combined-repair evaluations, six native comparison conditions,
saved-output replay and metric audits, manuscript numerical checks, and visual
inspection of every page are complete. Earlier study outputs and failures remain
preserved. Reproduction steps, exact-byte versus numerical-retraining limitations,
source hashes, models and predictions are retained in the supporting artifact.
