AUTHOR REVIEW GUIDE

Paper: Restoring Operational Histories with Synthetic Temporal Graph Supervision
Main manuscript: paper/main.pdf
Editable source: paper/main.tex
Reproduction commands: README.md

The application in easy English

A company records what happened to a loan application or purchase order. Some
records lose their object reference, attach to the wrong object, or use a second
identifier for the same object. An analyst then sees an incomplete or misleading
history. This paper studies learning to repair those connections while keeping
the recorded activities, times, cancellations and other event content intact.

The training idea

We execute small workflow worlds where the correct history is known. We vary how
the same execution is described: for example, fields can change with activities,
contain noisy measurements, or be sparse. Recording errors are applied separately.
These worlds teach a model which participation links to add/remove and which
identifier fragments to merge. The paper's principal contribution is this paired
training-data study and its application evidence. It does not introduce a new
neural architecture or claim a new general domain-randomization principle.

The strongest completed finding

Three paired training/fault trials were completed on each operational cohort.
Mean history-retrieval F1 is shown below. Higher is better.

                         Damaged input   Original training   Varied training
BPI 2017 loans/offers          0.787              0.791              0.805
BPI 2019 orders/items         0.760              0.809              0.826
BPI 2012 check                0.745              0.776              0.815

Every one of these nine paired comparisons favors varied training. On the 96
held-out workflow worlds, the three-trial mean improves from 0.759 to 0.800.
The public-source evaluation uses controlled recording errors; these are not
independently annotated naturally occurring errors.

Why the paper reports more than one score

Better average histories do not mean every history is correct. The loan result
recovers few identity pairs, and its independent pipeline-fault test gets slightly
worse. Purchasing produces false identity pairs in all three mixed-fault trials.
Some correct event assignments are damaged. Short histories can lose their only
correct record. Exact-history and preservation results, examples and failures are
therefore retained in the manuscript and machine-readable reports.

The selected boosted model is not the best architecture on every cohort.
GraphSAGE has higher trial-zero history F1 on loans and BPI 2012 check. A stable-
field operating configuration also performs better on BPI 2012, but causes severe
assignment damage on purchasing. The paper claims a benefit over matched original-
observation training, not universal superiority over every method or setting.

Calibration and supervised examples matter

The BPI 2012 source-supervised control reaches 0.878 on its same-process check
partition, above generated-only training. Its original loan transfer result is
poor, but an explicitly post-freeze sensitivity using already-frozen generated
calibration improves it from 0.547 to 0.792. That original failure cannot fairly be
attributed to the source-trained weights alone. The sensitivity uses no target
labels to fit new weights or tune a new threshold, and is reported separately
from the original predeclared comparisons.

All nine sensitivity runs are complete. Generated calibration changes purchasing
F1 only slightly (0.760 to 0.761) while almost eliminating new assignment damage.
On BPI 2012 check it lowers F1 from 0.878 to 0.825 while reducing mean new damage
from 495 to 52 events. Calibration therefore exposes a recovery/preservation
trade-off; no single operating point dominates across all applications.

Study scope and remaining publication risks

- All 84 original evaluations on the three available operational cohorts are
  complete. The generated study contains six model evaluations of 96 worlds in
  both damaged and clean conditions: 1,152 graph evaluations, not 1,152 independent
  training trials.
- Four additional public simulation sources were declared but could not be
  retrieved in this environment. Their 112 planned comparisons were not run.
  The original protocol, failed download record and exact omission remain visible.
- BatteryVault and ClaimStream are independent-simulator development evidence.
  Their results influenced method selection and are not presented as untouched
  confirmation datasets.
- There is no unchanged numerical reproduction of HERE, SANAGRAPH or the physical
  Event Knowledge Graph rules. Their native input/target assumptions differ.
  The manuscript and published-method audit explain the compatible controls.
- The main novelty is an application-focused supervision and evaluation protocol.
  A reviewer may ask for stronger native-method comparison or natural-error
  validation. Acceptance cannot be guaranteed by completing experiments.
- The three primary trials jointly vary training worlds and test faults. Reported
  ranges are descriptive; events are not treated as independent replications.

Reproducibility already checked

The relocated core passed 51 research/generator checks. All six primary models
reproduced the fixed first generated graph's metrics and exact edit program.
The 14 BPI 2012 tasks regenerated byte-identical observation/reference files.
A complete relocated mixed-task evaluation reproduced every edit, application
query, repaired graph and identity cluster. The check split also rebuilt exactly
from the raw public BPI 2012 source alone. These are same-environment replay and
conversion checks; they are not a claim of independent retraining on new hardware.

A final provenance audit identified eight historical archive checksum fields
captured during concurrent writes. Every member of all 60 source archives matches
its recorded per-file inventory; model, calibration and dataset hashes are
unaffected. Original reports are preserved, and source_snapshot_audit_v1.json
contains the explicit checksum errata. Atomic archive writing now prevents the
race, and the complete local suite passes 52 checks including its regression test.
