# Temporal Graph Repair

Research on restoring event participation and fragmented identities in operational histories, including the conditional value of generated workflow supervision.

- [Current paper](revision/paper/main.pdf) and [LaTeX source](revision/paper/main.tex).
- [Confirmation results](revision/confirmation_v3/RESULTS.txt), [trial table](revision/confirmation_v3/per_trial.csv), [protocol](revision/confirmation_v3/protocol.json), and [reproduction guide](revision/confirmation_v3/REPRODUCE.txt).
- [Supporting exploratory results](revision/improvement_v2/RESULTS.txt).
- Implementation: `tgrepair/`; experiment scripts: `scripts/` and `revision/`; tests: `tests/`.

The retained study is *When Generated Workflows Help Temporal Graph Repair: Verification Budgets and Merge Safety*. Generated supervision helped under one specified loan-data setting and performed worse in other settings. The paper preserves negative results, merge risks and the limitations of injected-error evaluation.

The separate temporal-walk counterfactual-audit project referenced in historical notes was not present in this folder and is not included.

## Public contents

This repository contains a curated selection of essential files. Raw datasets, downloaded literature PDFs, model checkpoints, caches, routine logs, temporary files, worker staging and redundant exports are omitted. The complete original project remains local. No full-backup Release is retained.

The [publication manifest](PUBLICATION_MANIFEST.json) records the included source files and their hashes. Existing research results and documents were copied without rerunning experiments or independently revalidating their scientific claims. Historical documents may refer to files that are available only in the local project.

Some saved-output audits and rebuild commands require omitted local inputs. Third-party material retains its original terms; no new blanket license is granted.
